# Reference analysis and design choices

DeskTime reference: https://desktime.com/features/ai-summary
- Uses an AI-led headline, trial/demo actions, process explanation, benefit sections and links to related features.
- Adapted the explanation-preview-benefits-workflow-FAQ-CTA sequence and broad feature discovery.
- ClockMetric copy describes observable work context. DeskTime-specific integrations, productivity recommendations, customer reviews and ratings are not represented as ClockMetric capabilities.

Potiva reference: https://potivaservices.com.au/
- Presents ecommerce services through prominent calls to action, service cards, business benefits and FAQs.
- Adapted the clear service-to-detail navigation and ecommerce/virtual-assistant use cases.
- No unverified testimonials, customer counts or performance metrics were copied.

ClockMetric implementation:
- Original orange and warm-white visual design with rounded borders, soft gradients and clear typography.
- Product-style visuals built in HTML/CSS; labelled sample data rather than generic stock imagery.
- AI summaries lead the hero, navigation and feature discovery, with a related AI CTA across feature pages.
- A$10 per active employee/month and 14-day trial are consistent throughout public marketing pages.
- Windows employee tracker and browser-based admin dashboard are clearly distinguished.
- This redesign changes the public website, not the separate tracking platform or AI backend.
